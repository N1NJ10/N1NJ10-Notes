Four frames deep.

One step = one instruction. The middle column is the machine: the CPU on top — instruction pointer, registers, ALU, flags — and RAM below it, where every slot is 8 bytes, high addresses at the top, and the stack grows downward as each call opens a frame. The strip between them is the bus: it shows whether this instruction moved anything between the two, and which direction. Most instructions never leave the chip. Companion to the cheat sheet.
